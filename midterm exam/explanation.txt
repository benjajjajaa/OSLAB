BB it’s the state field for every process.
In the CC struct the field BB JJ; holds the current state of the process.

BB is a simple state machine for each CC process.
It drives the simulation’s behaviour by indicating:

who should be on a queue (READY),
who is actively using the CPU (RUNNING),
who has been suspended waiting for resources (BLOCKED),
and who is done (TERMINATED).
